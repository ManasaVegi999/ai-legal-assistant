# AI Legal Assistant for Indian Law

An AI-powered legal assistance system designed to help users understand Indian laws and legal documents using Artificial Intelligence and Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG).

## 📌 Project Overview

The AI Legal Assistant helps users clarify their legal doubts by asking questions related to Indian law or uploading legal case documents.

It uses Google's Gemini 2.5 Flash model to generate relevant legal explanations based on retrieved legal information.

## ✨ Key Features

- **AI Legal Chatbot:** Ask questions related to Indian law.
- **Document Upload:** upload legal case files for analysis.
- **Legal Explanation:** Get easy-to-understand explanations of legal queries.
- **Case Analysis:** Analyze uploaded case documents.
- **User Authentication:** Secure user registration and login.
- **Admin Dashboard:** Manage legal categories.
- **Chat History:** Save and access previous queries.
- **PDF Download:** Download legal explanations.
